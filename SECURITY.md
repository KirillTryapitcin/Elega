# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/KirillTryapitcin/Elega/security/advisories/new),
not in public issues. Include steps to reproduce and the impact you expect. We aim to
acknowledge reports within three working days.

Elega stores personal data of real users. Do not test against production accounts other
than your own, and do not access, change or delete other people's data.
